Framework Explorer

See how five web design toolkits look and feel

How to use this

1
Browse. Open each framework page below. Notice the buttons, fonts, spacing and colors.
2
Play with the look. Click the round 🎨 button at the bottom-left of any framework page to change its fonts, colors and borders. Try the quick looks, or “Surprise me”.
3
Ask your AI. Copy one of the prompts at the bottom of each page into your AI coding agent and see what it makes.
4
Compare and repeat. Your AI saves each new design as a separate page, so nothing gets lost and you can always go back.

The five frameworks

Click a card to open that framework's demo page in a new tab.

Bootstrap

The most popular choice. Clean, familiar, works everywhere.

Feels like
A polished business website
Good for
Getting something solid built fast
Watch out
Sites can look "default" unless you customize them
Open Bootstrap →

Tailwind CSS

No ready-made look. You (or your AI) design every detail.

Feels like
A modern startup or app website
Good for
Unique, custom designs. AI agents are very good at it
Watch out
The page code looks busy, with lots of small style words
Open Tailwind →

Bulma

Friendly and simple, with readable class names.

Feels like
A bright, approachable small-business site
Good for
Simple sites where readable code matters
Watch out
Styles only: no built-in pop-ups or menus that move
Open Bulma →

Materialize

Based on Google's "Material Design" look.

Feels like
An Android app or an older Google product
Good for
A bold, colorful, app-like style with shadows and ripples
Watch out
The original project is no longer actively updated
Open Materialize →

Foundation

A professional toolkit built for flexible layouts.

Feels like
A corporate, news or institutional website
Good for
Structured, content-heavy pages
Watch out
Smaller community and slower updates these days
Open Foundation →
